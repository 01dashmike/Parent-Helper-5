## Script 2: "Baby Sensory Tips" (New Mums)

**Hook (0-3s):**
"Your baby's brain is developing faster than you think. Here's how to boost it with simple sensory play!"

**Visual:** Close-up of baby's face, then sensory activities

**Script:**
"First: High contrast. Black and white patterns are perfect for newborns. Their eyes can't see color yet, so bold patterns grab attention.

Second: Texture exploration. Let them touch different fabrics - silk, wool, cotton. Describe each one as they explore.

Third: Sound variety. Play different music, make sounds with household items. Their auditory system is wiring up fast.

Fourth: Movement. Gentle bouncing, swaying, or baby yoga classes help develop balance and coordination.

Want to find sensory classes near you? Search 'Parent Helper' - we've got classes for every age and stage."

## Script 3: "How to Pick the Right Class" (Dads)

**Hook (0-3s):**
"Picking a class for your kid? Don't waste money. Here's what actually matters."

**Visual:** Quick cuts of different classes, dad with kid

**Script:**
"Skip the fancy marketing. Here's what to check:

One: Age range. Is your kid actually in the right age group? Too young and they'll struggle. Too old and they'll be bored.

Two: Class size. Small groups mean more attention. Ask about ratios.

Three: Trial classes. Always do a trial first. If they won't let you try it, that's a red flag.

Four: Location. Is it actually convenient? You'll be going every week, so make sure it's doable.

Five: Your kid's interest. Don't force it. If they're not into it, find something else.

Use Parent Helper to compare classes side-by-side. Reviews, prices, and trial options all in one place."

**On-screen text:**
- "5 Things to Check"
- "Age Range"
- "Class Size"
- "Trial First!"

**CTA (25-30s):**
"Search classes near you on Parent Helper - compare and find the perfect fit!"

**Visual guidance:**
- Direct-to-camera talking
- Practical, no-nonsense vibe
- Show comparison screenshots
- Confident, helpful tone

## Script 5: "SEND-Friendly Activities" (SEND Families)

**Hook (0-3s):**
"Finding activities for kids with additional needs? Here's what to look for."

**Visual:** Inclusive activities, diverse kids, supportive environment

**Script:**
"First: Ask about accessibility. Can they accommodate your child's needs? Don't be afraid to ask specific questions.

Second: Small class sizes. More attention means better support. Look for classes with lower ratios.

Third: Sensory-friendly options. Many places offer adapted sessions with reduced noise, dimmer lights, or quieter environments.

Fourth: Trained staff. Ask if instructors have experience with SEND children. It makes a huge difference.

Fifth: Trial sessions. Always try before you commit. See if it's the right fit for your child.

Parent Helper has filters for SEND-friendly classes. Search by accessibility features and read reviews from other SEND families."

## General Production Tips

### Visual Guidelines:
- **Hook (0-3s):** Must grab attention immediately
- **Pacing:** Fast cuts, keep energy high
- **Lighting:** Bright, natural when possible
- **Audio:** Clear voiceover, upbeat music (royalty-free)
- **Text:** Large, readable fonts, on-screen for 2-3 seconds
- **Ending:** Always include Parent Helper logo/CTA

### Best Practices:
1. **First 3 seconds are critical** - hook must be strong
2. **Show, don't just tell** - visual demonstrations work best
3. **Keep it authentic** - real moments resonate more
4. **Include captions** - many watch without sound
5. **Use trending sounds** - but make sure they fit the content
6. **Post consistently** - 3-5 times per week for best results
7. **Engage with comments** - respond to build community

### CTA Variations:
- "Search classes near you on Parent Helper - link in bio!"
- "Find the perfect class on Parent Helper - search now!"
- "Book your free trial on Parent Helper - link in bio!"
- "Search [provider name] on Parent Helper and book today!"
